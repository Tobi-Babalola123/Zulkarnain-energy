Figma Make actually performs better with prompts that are **design-oriented first** and **implementation-oriented second**. Unlike v0, it tends to produce cleaner results if you describe the experience, layout, and interactions in detail rather than focusing heavily on the tech stack.

Here's a prompt optimized specifically for **Figma Make**.

---

# Figma Make Prompt

## ROLE

You are an award-winning Product Designer, Senior UI/UX Designer, CRO Specialist, and Frontend Designer.

Design and generate a complete, modern homepage for **Zulkarnain Energy Limited**, a Nigerian renewable energy company.

The current website is built with Wix and has an outdated appearance, weak conversion flow, poor visual hierarchy, repetitive sections, and lacks modern user experience principles.

This redesign should completely transform the website into a premium, high-converting landing page that immediately builds trust and encourages visitors to request a free solar consultation.

The design should feel comparable to websites from:

* Tesla Energy
* EcoFlow
* Sun King
* Arnergy
* Daystar Power

The overall experience should look like it was designed by a premium digital agency, with excellent spacing, modern typography, strong storytelling, and clear conversion paths.

---

# Design Style

Create a clean, modern, premium interface.

Use:

* Large bold typography
* Glassmorphism where appropriate
* Soft shadows
* Rounded cards
* Plenty of whitespace
* Smooth gradients
* Premium photography
* Modern icons
* Elegant hover states
* Scroll reveal animations
* Animated counters
* Beautiful card layouts
* Consistent spacing throughout
* A bright and trustworthy aesthetic

Avoid:

* Wix template styling
* Generic layouts
* Small fonts
* Dense paragraphs
* Image sliders
* Outdated UI components

# User Experience Goals

The homepage should guide visitors through a clear conversion journey:

Hero → Trust → Benefits → Services → Projects → Savings Calculator → Process → Why Choose Us → Team → Community Impact → Statistics → Testimonials → FAQ → Contact.

Every section should naturally encourage visitors to request a quote.

---

# Interactions

Include:

* Sticky navigation
* Smooth scrolling
* Scroll-triggered fade and slide animations
* Hover effects on buttons and cards
* Animated statistics
* Image zoom on hover
* Button ripple effects
* Accessible focus states
* Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile

---

# Final Goal

Generate a polished, production-ready homepage that feels like a **high-end renewable energy company website**, prioritizing trust, clarity, storytelling, and lead generation over simply showcasing products. The design should immediately communicate expertise and make it effortless for visitors to request a consultation or quotation.
